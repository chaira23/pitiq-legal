Delete your PitIQ data

Last updated: October 9, 2026

In the app (fastest)

Open PitIQ → More → Data & privacy → "Delete my data and reset". This deletes your account and the data linked to it (cloud team, leagues, scores, predictions, alert token, and any team history you opted in to). It also clears the data stored on your device.

By email

If you linked an email address to your PitIQ account, write to tozankayra4@gmail.com from that address and we will delete the data linked to it and confirm by reply.

PitIQ works without an account, and an anonymous account has no name or email that would let us tell which data is yours. For that reason deleting inside the app is the only reliable way for anonymous accounts; if you can no longer open the app, reinstalling it and using the button above will not find the old anonymous account, so write to us and we will help as far as the data allows.

What this does not do

Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings (or in Google Play on Android).

Some records are kept after deletion, as described in the Privacy Policy: the purchase record held by RevenueCat, proof that you agreed to or withdrew the optional team-history consent (kept 24 months, no team data), and feedback you sent, which is kept for up to 90 days with its link to your account removed.

Advertising data held by Meta

If you allowed tracking, Meta may hold advertising-measurement data about your device under its own retention. You can manage that in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings, and you can switch tracking off for PitIQ at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
