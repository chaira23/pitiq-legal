Build from your budget

Choose a build strategy (optimal, value or premium) and a chip mode. PitIQ checks every valid lineup of five drivers and two constructors and shows the best one. The result can be shared as a card.

Chip modes

The standard and wildcard modes are free. Limitless mode removes the budget cap so you can see the true best team, and is part of Pro.

Compare with the optimum

With Pro you can also compare your own team with the optimal one and see exactly which swaps would add the most.
